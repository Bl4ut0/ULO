Universal Layer Operator (ULO) Changelog
========================================

v1.0.0 (2026-11-14)
- Initial release supporting WoW Forever, Classic Era, TBC Anniversary, Mists Classic, and Retail.
- Added GUID/UnitToken layer detection, 180s refresh countdown & safe reload deferral, and post-hop cooldown tracking.
- Added automated invite coordination, AutoLayer/Overlord/NWB interoperability, and safe non-hop party invite handling.
- Added custom ULO bronze astrolabe textures, orbital minimap badge, movable status HUD with Refresh/Hop buttons, and Blizzard Settings panel.
